Things to remember

  • always use fieldset after form tag in form
  • Use legend for give title to form

  • Always use text area for address and also describe rows and cols
  • every id and for should be change only name can be same
  • use required attribute to important field
  • minlength and maxlegth should be also used in every posible field
  • Add required to one radio button in the group (or all of them, but one is enough
  • If you want at least one checkbox to be selected, add required to one checkbox in the group
